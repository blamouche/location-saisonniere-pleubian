The house at a glance

Maison Od Joge is a Breton stone house for 8 people, in Pleubian (22610), on the wild peninsula between the Trieux and Jaudy estuaries. Enclosed garden, wood stove, a dedicated desk, the beach, and the Talbert sand spit a few minutes away.

Right now on the peninsula — spring

The light returns (1 March – 31 May)

  • Follow the spring tides at the Talbert sand spit

    The nature reserve reveals its whole shoreline at low tide — one of the best times to walk it.

  • Watch migrating birds

    The wild peninsula is a stopover on the spring migration route.

  • Walk the GR34 before the summer crowds

    The customs officers’ path, still quiet, with the light returning.

  • Enjoy low-season rates

    Before high season, availability and prices are more flexible.

  • See the gorse in bloom on the moorland

    Gorse’s bright yellow marks the Breton landscape in spring.

Right now on the peninsula — summer

Eight around a table, the sea five minutes away (1 June – 31 August)

  • Swim at Kermagen beach

    A family beach with a boat ramp, minutes from the house.

  • Eat outdoors in the enclosed garden

    Eight around a table, sheltered, steps from the kitchen.

  • Paddle toward the Bréhat archipelago

    Kayak or sail, depending on the day’s tides and wind.

  • Discover the Talbert sand spit early in the morning

    Before the heat and the crowds, the pebble spit is worth the early start.

  • Cove-hop

    Port-la-Chaîne, Port-Béni, Pors Rand: quieter anchorages than the big beaches.

Right now on the peninsula — autumn

The wind, the fire, the silence (1 Sept – 30 Nov)

  • Watch the sea rise during the equinox storms

    The spectacle of the big autumn tides, from the coast.

  • Walk the GR34 without meeting a soul

    The peninsula finds its calm again — the customs officers’ path all to yourself.

  • Light the wood stove after a walk

    The house’s star feature outside summer.

  • Enjoy the low rates of the late season

    The quietest time of year to book.

  • Visit Tréguier in peace and quiet

    The Saint-Tugdual cathedral and the historic town, without the summer rush.

Right now on the peninsula — winter

Warm and cosy, far from everything (1 Dec – end of February)

  • Disconnect completely

    No notifications, just the stove, the sea and the rhythm of the tides.

  • Work remotely from the dedicated desk

    WiFi and a quiet desk — the house is built for a workation.

  • Watch the starry sky

    Far from the light pollution of big cities.
