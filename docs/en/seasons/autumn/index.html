The essentials

In autumn (1 Sept – 30 Nov), Pleubian's wild peninsula offers 10 main activities around the Talbert sand spit and the GR34 trail. Maison Od Joge (8 guests, wood stove, enclosed garden) serves as a base camp 5 minutes à pied from the sea.
