Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum length of stay?

To be confirmed with the hosts — generally shorter outside peak season.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in at 15:00 (low season) or 17:00 (high season), check-out before 10:00 (to be confirmed).

Is a security deposit required?

Deposit details are provided at the time of booking.

Is end-of-stay cleaning included in the rate?

Yes, end-of-stay cleaning is included.

Is the tourist tax included?

Amount and collection method to be published before launch (legal requirement, PRD §15.2).

How do I actually book?

Via the Airbnb / Booking buttons above, which link to the official booking platform.

Can I cancel my booking?

Cancellation terms are those of the platform used to book (Airbnb or Booking).

Are pets allowed?

No, pets are not allowed.
